# UX and motion design — v3.4

**Gate G2** after TCL Home/recovery reliability; before G3 media expansion. The experience is calm, original, cinematic and remote-first, with a six-app editable dock, Apps grid, clear focus and readable 10-foot UI. Active Home uses animated radial mesh; the separate Library browse/detail uses relevant title art; idle Wallpaper uses artwork/photos. No photographic wallpaper behind the active dock. Keep navigation focus predictable and no first-key loss on waking.

## Single focus timing contract

- **On D-pad focus change:** begin crossfade **immediately** (no 110–160 ms settle delay), **400 ms** duration, cancellable/re-targetable on rapid repeated focus changes. Any previous 135 ms delay/380 ms dissolve is legacy behaviour to replace or flag as noncompliant.
- Background light fields: 2–4 **small pre-rendered/cached gradient textures**, moved/scaled with GPU compositor; **no full-screen gradient redraw at 60 FPS**. Gentle drift amplitude **2–5% of the light field's own size**, cycle **20–40 seconds**; paused or static when app backgrounded, on low performance, reduced motion, idle or accessibility preference. These are design targets subject to TCL performance testing; throttle to static when necessary.
- Six palettes: SmartTube crimson/charcoal, Netflix deep red/black, Disney+ navy/indigo/cyan, BBC magenta/pink, Live TV violet/indigo, Music peach/coral/pink. Reference images at `../references/ambient/01-smarttube.jpg` through `06-music.jpg` are **visual references**, not redistributable production assets.
- Frosted-glass panels should remain readable, use GPU-compatible compositing, avoid expensive full-screen blur on TCL Android 11. Focus contrast, safe area, motion preference, remote navigation and frame-time/latency measurements are required.

## Wallpaper mode

Default: curated licensed artwork with offline fallback, user can opt into personal photos later. Idle enters Wallpaper, remote interaction returns to Active Home. Clock style/position/visibility configurable. `Auto` depth clock uses **prepared, quality-checked foreground masks** for specifically eligible images; no TV-side real-time ML segmentation requirement. Landscapes/abstracts generally show simple/no clock; this is expected, not a defect. Selected masks should visibly occlude the clock behind the subject without artefacts. Protect OLED with dimming, motion/timeouts and reduced-motion. Never alter Samsung native Art Mode.

## Art sourcing and licensing

Use explicitly free-for-intended-use/public-domain/owner-licensed artwork; record creator, URL/source, licence, attribution, redistribution/bundling rights, image dimensions and mask provenance. **Do not equate “on Unsplash” with unrestricted redistribution rights.** No external downloads required for offline Wallpaper. Avoid uncontrolled 4K app-size growth: cap curated starter assets, cache/downsample to TV resolution, report APK/WGT size budget before bundling. Images supplied as design references are not automatically licensed wallpaper assets.

## Reference acceptance

Ambient references actually packaged under `references/ambient/01-smarttube.jpg` ... `06-music.jpg`; wallpaper references `references/wallpaper/wallpaper-reference-01.png` ... `05.png`. Compare broad composition, focus and palette, not pixel-perfect copies. AM-01 cannot be passed without image and actual rendered TV evidence. No unverified Apple proprietary depth/segmentation API.

## v3.4 minimal Home and Settings

Home must **not** show hero film posters, Continue Watching rows, recommendations or universal search by default. Keep the editable six-app dock, ambient mesh, optional clock and restrained status. A separate Library screen holds content. Settings uses a D-pad-first two-column rail/detail layout, large focus targets, clear Back and accessible previews; see module 12.

## v3.4.1 — Seamless vertical Home navigation (owner-confirmed)

**NAV-01 — One continuous Home surface.** Home is a single focus graph with three vertically adjacent zones: **Utilities above**, **six-app dock in the centre**, and **NAS films/Library below**. The default viewport remains minimal and centred on the dock; adjacent zones are revealed by D-pad movement, not persistent extra panels or modal menus. No new intermediate page, menu, splash or confirmation for moving between zones.

**NAV-02 — UP from dock.** A single Up press moves focus into the Utilities row and reveals its icons directly. NordVPN and AirPin are owner examples, **not universal hardcoded defaults**. The row is populated from detected installed utilities and configurable shortcuts. Left/Right moves along utilities; OK launches the focused utility. **Remove the existing Up-triggered menu from this Home context.** Other screens may keep their own contextual navigation. If no utility exists, provide a safe, discoverable alternative (e.g. Settings entry) rather than a dead focus trap.

**NAV-03 — DOWN from dock.** A single Down press reveals NAS films / Library and places focus on the first meaningful content tile (prefer Continue Watching when present, otherwise recently added/first available). Horizontal D-pad moves through the current content row; further Down can traverse library sections. This is a *seamless reveal of the separate Library content area*, not an always-visible content row on minimal Home. Back/Up returns to the dock predictably.

**NAV-04 — Continuous motion and focus.** Keep the same animated background and visual context through vertical transitions. Use GPU-friendly slide/scroll and focus animations with no artificial delay, no black flash, and no unnecessary loading screen. Existing immediate 400 ms focus colour crossfade remains in force. Rapid repeated presses must not lose input, duplicate transitions or land on invisible focus targets. Honour reduced-motion and low-performance fallbacks.

**NAV-05 — Empty and unavailable states.** No NAS configured, temporarily offline, empty library, Kids restrictions, VPN unavailable or AirPin not installed must not break directional navigation. Down opens a friendly Library empty/reconnect state without requesting an IP; Up shows only actual configured shortcuts. Provide discoverable access to Settings without hijacking Up.

**NAV-06 — Focus restoration and platform constraints.** Back from a revealed zone returns to the previous focus/dock; launching and returning from an app restores the last logical Home focus where supported. TV/system-reserved buttons remain untouched. On Android, opening a third-party app may cause a platform-managed transition; do not claim uninterrupted background animation while outside Unified TV. Samsung native Smart Hub/Art Mode must remain intact.

**NAV-07 — Settings.** In Appearance & Home → Navigation/Shortcuts, configure Utilities row visibility/order, available NordVPN/AirPin shortcuts, preferred first Library row, and optional direct-access Settings shortcut. **D-pad directions themselves are fixed** in the default navigation model to preserve muscle memory. Do not allow a configuration that strands the user.

**NAV-08 — Physical acceptance.** On each target TV test single Up, single Down, rapid repeated Up/Down, Left/Right in each zone, OK launch, Back return, missing utility, offline NAS, empty Kids library, sleep/wake, return from external apps, and reduced motion. Record video and remote-input/focus timing evidence. This is a new requirement, **not an already verified implementation**.

## v3.4.2 — Mandatory minimalist app dock glyphs (owner-confirmed)

**UX-ICON-01 — Icon visual language.** Each service must be represented by a minimalist, monochromatic, or simplified brand glyph/icon (e.g., a simple 'N' for Netflix, or a minimalist play button for SmartTube) rather than a plain text lockup or a heavy, full-color app square. This ensures visual harmony within the frosted glass dock and prevents icons from competing with the dynamic ambient mesh. If an official minimalist mark is unavailable, design a clean, universally recognizable vector symbol for the service. **Do not fall back to text-only tiles.**

**UX-ICON-02 — Focus and labels.** Selected tile gains a slight lift/scale, crisp luminous outline and soft coloured halo. **The tiles themselves must use minimalist, simplified icons rather than text lockups or full-colour standard Android/Tizen icons.** Unfocused tiles remain legible, not washed out or blanked. Dock labels are separate from the icon tiles and follow the configurable visibility policy in UX-ICON-05; the default is **Focused only**. The selected app title above the dock may update with focus where the approved composition provides it, without duplicating labels unnecessarily.

**UX-ICON-03 — Assets, identity and accessibility.** Prefer official simplified brand assets only where their licensing and redistribution terms permit. Otherwise use an original, non-infringing, non-misleading vector symbol; do not copy protected artwork or assume any arbitrary glyph is legally safe. Consistent silhouette, stroke/optical weight, padding, and contrast across six dock slots; scalable vectors or platform-equivalent assets; readable at ten-foot distance; light/dark mesh contrast; focus and reduced-motion states. A household may rearrange apps without losing icon style. Installed app icons may be used for discovery in Apps/Settings but must not be displayed as heavy full-colour dock tiles.

**UX-ICON-04 — Acceptance.** Verify all six dock slots and editable replacements on TCL, Sony and Samsung: no plain-text icon tiles, no default full-colour app squares, clear focus/labels, correct launch mapping, icon rendering at TV distance, accessibility contrast, no performance regression during animated mesh transitions. Track implementation and physical TV evidence separately in `docs/ACCEPTANCE_REGISTER.csv`.


## UX-UTILITY — Top-right frosted-glass utility pill (v3.4.3 mandatory)

**UX-UTILITY-01 — Visual contract.** On active Home, render **one compact frosted-glass pill in the top-right** containing, in order, **AirPlay status, NordVPN state, AI voice assistant, and Settings**. Use **minimalist, text-free white glyphs only**: AirPlay/cast glyph, VPN shield, microphone, gear. No flags, text lockups, coloured app squares, duplicated top-right menu, or permanently expanded labels. Icons must have uniform optical size, consistent stroke/weight, adequate spacing, and readable contrast against the animated mesh. Brand marks must respect licensing; use a neutral, accurate capability glyph where necessary.

**UX-UTILITY-02 — Truthful state.** AirPlay/AirPin status must not imply a receiver is running if it is not installed, permitted, or connected. NordVPN shield must not imply protection unless a verified VPN connection is active; distinguish disconnected, connecting, verified, and unknown with subtle accessible treatments while keeping the normal pill monochrome. The AI microphone must not imply active listening unless it is actually listening, with an explicit consent/permission path. Unsupported features should be omitted or safely disabled with explanation on focus, not fabricated.

**UX-UTILITY-03 — Interaction.** **Single Up from the six-app dock focuses the pill directly**, without opening an intermediate menu. Left/Right moves through its four focusable icons; OK activates the focused action or relevant detail, Back returns to the dock. On focus, a short accessible label may appear **outside the icon** or through a focus tooltip; the glyph itself remains text-free. D-pad focus order, Back restoration and reduced-motion settings apply. The configurable utility shortcut mapping remains available in Settings; this visual grouping must not hardcode NordVPN or AirPin for every household.

**UX-UTILITY-04 — Motion and interruption.** Pill is **visually silent during normal browsing**: no looping pulse, animated status, or intrusive pop-up. Expand/animate only for a **critical, verified state change** requiring user action, e.g. protected Live TV VPN verification failure. A mere focus change gets the normal restrained focus outline, not a critical alert. Emergency smoke/CO alerts always outrank pill activity. Hide the pill in gallery/ambient mode, Samsung native Art Mode and third-party full-screen apps, preserving platform restrictions.

**UX-UTILITY-05 — Acceptance.** Verify on TCL Android TV, Sony Google TV and Samsung Frame: default/focused appearance over moving mesh; icon contrast; Up/Left/Right/OK/Back; receiver absent; VPN connected/disconnected/unknown; microphone permission denied; Settings access; critical-state expansion; ambient/Art Mode absence; emergency priority. Record platform limitations and physical-TV evidence in the existing acceptance register. These are requirements, not claims of implementation.


## v3.4.4 — Approved Home visual and dock label behaviour (owner-confirmed)

**UX-ICON-05 — Configurable label visibility.** Under **Settings → Appearance & Home → Dock & Icons → App label visibility**, offer exactly these three values: **Focused only (DEFAULT)**, **Always show labels**, and **Hide labels**. The initial installation default is **Focused only**, for both Adults and Kids; each profile may independently override it. In Focused only, render a readable app-name label outside/beneath the icon tile **only for the currently focused app**, update it immediately when focus changes, and remove the previous label cleanly without shifting tile geometry. In Always show labels, render all visible app labels (up to the configured six or eight); in Hide labels, render no per-tile labels but retain focus cues, accessible names, and discoverable app identity through supported focus/title or assistive mechanisms. A focused app must be identifiable even when labels are hidden; if a platform cannot achieve that accessibly, offer a clear warning or enforce a readable focus title. No label setting may substitute text-only tiles for mandatory minimalist glyphs. Never duplicate a label above and below the dock without an intentional, documented composition rule.

**UX-ICON-06 — Persistence and configuration.** Store `dock.labelVisibility` as a versioned per-profile enum `focused_only | always | hidden`, default `focused_only`; validate/migrate missing or unknown values to `focused_only` without losing other settings. Changes preview immediately, persist across restart, work on six-app dock and edited app choices, and are remote-accessible with clear selected-state indication. Profile switch applies the target profile's setting without flashing stale labels. Kids can use Always show without changing Adults. Reset appearance restores Focused only. No unnecessary animation on reduced-motion.

**UX-VISUAL-01 — Primary approved Home reference.** `references/approved-home/Unified-TV-Approved-Home-2026-10-25.jpg` is the **owner-approved primary visual authority** for active Home composition, dock placement/proportions, frosted glass, clock, upper-right utility pill, atmospheric red/charcoal mesh and premium restraint. Earlier ambient/palette images remain **secondary references** for focused palettes, not replacements for this composition. The approved reference depicts always-visible labels, but **UX-ICON-05's explicit Focused-only default supersedes that aspect of the screenshot**. Likewise the image's Disney+/BBC wordmarks are conceptual and must be adapted to UX-ICON-01 minimalist-glyph policy. Functional, safety, accessible contrast, and platform feasibility contracts supersede any conflicting visual detail. The source image is a design reference, **not automatically licensed for redistribution as app artwork**.

**UX-VISUAL-02 — Acceptance.** Compare same-resolution TCL screenshot against the primary reference; document differences in proportions, background, glass treatment, focus and icon optical weight. Test all three label modes, all six dock slots, rapid focus changes, profile switching, settings persistence after restart, reduced motion and readability at normal viewing distance. Repeat platform-specific acceptance on Sony and Samsung. Do not mark physically verified without real-TV evidence.

## v3.4.5 — Entertainment builder icon and dock behaviour

**UX-ICON-07 — SmartTube.** SmartTube is represented by a simple YouTube-style *play* glyph, but the artwork must be original/appropriately licensed and **visibly distinct from the official YouTube icon**. Keep SmartTube's real app identity and accessible name. If both services appear in Apps or the dock, users must be able to tell them apart. No full-colour stock square or text-only fallback.

**UX-BUILD-01 — Setup aesthetics.** Entertainment Hub Builder follows the approved Home reference's restrained frosted-glass visual language, uses large remote-friendly category cards and clearly focused choices, and never overlays the animated Home with a permanent setup banner. Its selection screen may show service names and explanatory text outside the icon tiles; the Home dock itself remains minimalist with Focused-only labels by default. A category Yes/No question must be answerable in a few D-pad presses. Native store handoff and return must preserve selection/focus where possible.


## v3.4.6 — Configurable six- or eight-icon dock (owner-confirmed)

**UX-DOCK-08 — Dock capacity.** Default **6** slots; optional **8** slots under **Settings → Appearance & Home → Dock & Icons → Dock size**. Expose only `6 icons` and `8 icons` initially. The number of slots is a *capacity*, not a mandate to fill them. Changing 6 → 8 must not auto-populate unwanted apps; changing 8 → 6 must show a remote-operable preview and request explicit confirmation of which two shortcuts are removed from the visible dock (preserve their installed status and app preferences, never uninstall). Support cancel without data loss. Store `dock.capacity` per profile as versioned enum `6 | 8`, default 6, preserving Adults/Kids independence. A profile switch must not flash the wrong layout.

**UX-DOCK-09 — Responsive composition.** Both capacities must fit in one centered frosted-glass dock, without horizontal scrolling or clipping. For eight icons, adjust dock width and tile/spacing proportionately while preserving the approved Home's optical balance, safe-area margins, glass effects, restrained lift/halo, and readable focus identity. Do not merely force eight full-size tiles into the six-tile container. Keep Up directly to the utility pill, Down directly to Library/NAS, Left/Right across populated slots, Back focus restoration, and sensible focus when a slot is empty or removed. Support all three label modes; **Focused only remains the default**. With Always show, handle eight labels and long names without collisions (ellipsis/focus expansion or a tested layout). Use ten-foot readability, overscan, reduced-motion and RTL/locale checks.

**UX-DOCK-10 — Acceptance.** Test 6 default, 6→8 empty additional slots, manual addition of two apps, 8→6 confirmation/cancel, reorder, uninstall, full and partially filled docks, multi-service music apps on separate tiles, profile switching, reboot persistence, labels in all modes, focus navigation and platform-specific safe areas. Compare TCL preview screenshots against approved reference for both layouts; Sony and Samsung require separate real-TV evidence. Do not mark hardware-tested without actual device results.


## v3.4.7 — Settings overlay visual contract

**UX-GLASS-01:** On OK of the top-right Settings gear, slide a dark, translucent, Apple Liquid Glass-inspired Unified TV glass sheet **over** the stationary Home from the right, covering ~50% of screen. Home must not translate, shrink, reflow or move. Widen overlay to ~70–80% for complex tasks. Restrained blur/refraction, luminous hairline edge, readable text, remote focus, reduced-motion/static blur fallback. No opaque sidebar or split-screen. **Module 13 is authoritative** for tokens, all ten category screens, navigation, platform limitations and acceptance.
