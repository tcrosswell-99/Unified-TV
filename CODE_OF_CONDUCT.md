# Unified TV community code of conduct

Unified TV is a community project being made for a real family first. It should be welcoming to people who are new to Android TV development and to those who just want their television to work.

## What we expect

- Be respectful, patient and clear, especially when someone reports a bug on unfamiliar hardware.
- Prefer reproducible evidence to confident claims. Mention the television model, firmware and exactly what you tested.
- Respect original authors and licence terms. Give credit when discussing designs, code or hardware research.
- Make room for accessibility needs and for simpler, less technical installation paths.
- Keep other people's home networks, accounts and photographs private.

## Not allowed

Harassment, hateful or sexualised attacks, spam, impersonation, deliberate device damage, publishing private information, or presenting unsupported/unverified integrations as working.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit, close or remove disruptive material and restrict contributions. Report sensitive security concerns via [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) rather than public posts.

This document builds on the spirit of the upstream project's community standards, with independent Unified TV maintenance.
